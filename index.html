Hybrid - Cryo

Autonomous Hybrid Sensor-Driven Organ Transportation Cooling System

Developed by Team Micro-Mavericks from Bannari Amman Institute of Technology in collaboration with C-DAC. Our mission is to replace fragile ice-coolers and multimillion-dollar commercial perfusion equipment with an affordable, technician-free autonomous platform powered by the indigenous VEGA ET1031 RISC-V SoC.

The Conventional Problem

  • Traditional organ transport relies on basic ice-coolers with high rates of freezing injury or thermal overshoot.
  • Commercial continuous-perfusion systems cost between $25,000 to $65,000 per transit and mandate dedicated clinical perfusionists.
  • Zero tamper-evident proof of transit shocks, lid-opening, or temperature spikes creates legal liabilities and graft loss.

Our VEGA-Driven Innovation

  • Autonomous Dual-Loop Thermoregulation: Precision PID Peltier chilling with Thermoelectric Generator (TEG) energy harvesting.
  • Indigenous RISC-V Edge Intelligence: The VEGA ET1031 core executes concurrent FreeRTOS tasks for IMU dead reckoning, tamper detection, and hardware safety watchdog arbitration.
  • Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Custody: Every thermal excursion, lid-opening, and shock spike is cryptographically hashed for indisputable clinical audits.

Hybrid - Cryo Cold-Chain Services

Autonomous, technician-free organ logistics powered by continuous edge-sensing, active thermoelectric preservation, and immutable telemetry.

Active Dynamic Thermoregulation

Maintains the organ chamber within strict $0.0^\circ\text{C}$ to $4.0^\circ\text{C}$ boundaries using precision PID-controlled Peltier thermoelectric heat pumping. Eliminates freezing crystallization and heat spikes.

Tamper-Proof Chain of Custody

Continuous cryptographic SHA-256 event chaining logging all lid accesses, IMU shock impacts, and temperature excursions with hardware RTC timestamps for strict medical malpractice defense.

Automated Thermal Protection

Autonomous dual-zone thermal safeguards with independent hardware watchdog override and temperature excursion containment.
